This report presents the financial results for the fourth quarter ending December 31, 2024. All figures are expressed in millions of euros unless otherwise stated. The Board of Directors approved this report on February 10, 2025.

Revenue for Q4 reached €1,842M, representing an increase of 12.4% compared to the same period last year. Operating expenses grew at a lower rate of 8.1%, resulting in an improvement of the operating margin from 21.3% to 24.6%.

Net profit attributable to shareholders amounted to €312M, up from €247M in Q4 2023. Earnings per share increased to €2.18, compared to €1.73 in the prior year period. The company generated free cash flow of €289M during the quarter, reflecting strong operational performance across all business segments.

Capital expenditure totalled €94M in Q4, primarily directed towards the expansion of our manufacturing facility in Lyon and the ongoing digital transformation programme. The Group's net debt position improved to €521M at year-end, down from €638M at the end of 2023, resulting in a leverage ratio of 1.4x EBITDA.

Looking ahead to fiscal year 2025, management expects continued growth in revenue in the range of 8–11%, supported by new product launches in Q2 and the consolidation of the recently acquired Merano Technologies subsidiary. The Board has proposed a final dividend of €0.85 per share, subject to shareholder approval at the Annual General Meeting scheduled for April 2025.

In the DACH region, Q4 revenue grew 15.2% year-on-year to €487M, driven by strong demand in the industrial automation segment. The Benelux and Nordic markets combined contributed €391M, reflecting moderate growth of 6.8% against a challenging comparison base from the prior year.
